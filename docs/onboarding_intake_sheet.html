Calloway — Discovery Call Intake Sheet Operator reference for onboarding new real estate agent clients

Step 1: Agent Profile

FieldReq?What to AskDefinition
Full NameYes“What name do you go by with clients?”Display name the AI uses when signing messages. Should match their brand (e.g. “Sarah Mitchell” not “S. Mitchell”).
EmailYes“Best email for system notifications?”Receives error alerts, weekly reports, and account recovery. Not shared with clients.
PhoneYes“What’s your cell number?”Agent’s personal number in +1XXXXXXXXXX format. Where the AI sends command-channel SMS and escalation alerts.
BrokerageNo“Which brokerage are you with?”Firm name (e.g. “Keller Williams Realty”). Used in the AI’s context so it knows the agent’s affiliation.
Market AreaNo“What’s your primary area?”Geographic market (e.g. “Philadelphia Metro”). Helps the AI give location-aware responses.
TimezoneNo“Which timezone are you in?”Eastern / Central / Mountain / Pacific. Controls briefing times, after-hours rules, and scheduling. Default: Eastern.

Step 2: Communication Style

FieldReq?What to AskDefinition
ToneNo“How would you describe your texting style — polished, warm, or laid back?”Professional: “Good afternoon, I’d be happy to schedule that.” Friendly: “Hey! Absolutely, let’s get that set up.” Casual: “Sure thing! When works best?”
Emoji UsageNo“Do you use emojis when you text clients?”Yes or No. If yes, AI mirrors light emoji usage. If no, messages stay text-only.
Greeting StyleNo“How do you usually open a text?”AI’s default opening pattern (e.g. “Hi Sarah!” or “Hey there”). Blank = match tone naturally.
Sign-off StyleNo“How do you usually sign off?”Closing line the AI appends (e.g. “Best, Sarah” or “Talk soon!”). Blank = no sign-off.
Communication NotesNo“Anything else about how you talk to clients?”Free-text notes baked into the system prompt. Examples: “Never say ‘no problem’”, “Call properties ‘homes’ not ‘houses’”.
Pro tip: Ask them to forward 3–5 recent client text threads. Read them before filling this section — you’ll nail the voice much faster.

Step 3: Scheduling & Availability

FieldReq?What to AskDefinition
Showing DurationNo“How long are your typical showings?”30 / 45 / 60 / 90 minutes. AI blocks this on the calendar per showing. Default: 60 min.
Buffer TimeNo“How much travel time between back-to-back showings?”15 / 30 / 45 / 60 minutes. Prevents bookings too close together. Default: 30 min.
Earliest ShowingNo“What’s the earliest you’d do a showing?”Time of day (e.g. 9:00 AM). AI won’t book before this.
Latest ShowingNo“Latest you’d start a showing?”Time of day (e.g. 6:00 PM). AI won’t book after this.
After-Hours ModeNo“When a client texts at 11pm, what should happen?”Acknowledge & Queue: AI says “Thanks! [Agent] will follow up in the morning.” Handle Normally: AI responds 24/7. Silent: No reply; logged for morning briefing.
Briefing TimeNo“What time do you want your daily summary?”Time in their timezone (e.g. 7:30 AM). AI sends overnight activity, upcoming showings, pending items. Default: 7:30 AM.

Step 4: Autonomy & Trust Level

FieldReq?What to AskDefinition
Operating ModeNo“How much should the AI do on its own at first?”Supervised Week 1 rec.: AI drafts, agent approves. Proactive: Routine auto, sensitive asks. Autonomous: Fully independent. Command Only: Agent triggers manually.
Escalate: PricingNo“Loop you in on pricing/negotiations?”AI escalates pricing and offer questions. Default: Yes.
Escalate: LegalNo“Same for legal or contract questions?”AI flags legal, contract, disclosure topics. Default: Yes.
Escalate: UnhappyNo“When a client seems frustrated?”AI detects negative sentiment, escalates. Default: Yes.
Escalate: PersonalNo“Personal or emotional conversations?”AI escalates sensitive personal topics. Default: Off.
Escalate: New LeadNo“New unknown number texts in?”AI notifies agent on first-ever message from unknown. Default: Off.
Notification MethodNo“How do you want alerts?”SMS / Push (Firebase) / Both.
Notification FrequencyNo“Instant or batched?”Immediate: Every event. Batched (default): Every 30 min. Briefing Only: Daily digest only.

Step 5: Active Listings

Repeat for each listing. Can be skipped and added later from the tenant detail page.

FieldReq?What to AskDefinition
AddressNo“Full address?”Street, city, state, zip. AI uses for directions and property identification.
PriceNo“List price?”Current asking price. Accepts $450,000 or 450000.
BedsNo“Bedrooms?”Bedroom count.
BathsNo“Bathrooms?”Bathroom count (supports halves like 2.5).
SqftNo“Square footage?”Living area square footage.
StatusNo“Active, pending, or coming soon?”Active: On market. Pending: Under contract. Coming Soon: Not yet on MLS.
Showing InstructionsNo“Any special showing instructions?”“Call 30 min before”, “Occupied — knock first”, “Use back entrance.”
Lockbox CodeNo“Lockbox code?”Shared with verified agents for confirmed showings. Stored securely.

Step 6: Active Clients

Repeat for each client. Can be skipped and bulk-imported later. During handoff, agent introduces AI to 2–3 of these first.

FieldReq?What to AskDefinition
NameNo“Client’s name?”How the AI addresses them. Can be a couple: “Mike & Lisa Chen.”
PhoneNo“Their cell number?”+1XXXXXXXXXX format. How AI matches incoming texts to known clients.
EmailNo“Email?”For email-based communications or records.
RoleNo“Buyer, seller, or both?”Buyer / Seller / Both / Lead (not yet client) / Past Client. Determines which AI scenarios activate.
StageNo“Where are they in the process?”New Lead → Active Search → Showings Scheduled → Offer Pending → Under Contract → Closed
NotesNo“Anything the AI should know?”Free text: “Pre-approved $500K”, “Looking in Fishtown”, “Weekend showings only.”

Step 7: Channel Setup

FieldReq?What to AskDefinition
Twilio NumberYesOperator provisions — not a client questionDedicated number clients text to reach the AI. Must be provisioned in Twilio first. +1XXXXXXXXXX.
Google Review LinkNo“Do you have a Google Business review link?”URL to Google review page (g.page/r/…). AI can share with satisfied clients post-closing.
System Prompt OverrideNoOperator-only — advancedCustom instructions replacing default AI prompt. Leave blank 99% of the time. Only for edge cases the style/autonomy settings don’t cover.
Summary: 34 total fields across 7 steps. Only 4 required (name, email, phone, Twilio number). Everything else has sensible defaults. Average discovery call: 30–45 minutes.
